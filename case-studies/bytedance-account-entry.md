# Account Entry & AI-assisted Recovery

> Sanitized project-based internship case study. No internal system, employer document or proprietary prototype is published.

## Internship-period delivery

- Wrote an account-entry PRD covering registration, login and password recovery, with 10 functional points and usability, performance, compatibility and security requirements.
- Defined risk responses and reviewed the proposed interface direction with the manager.

## Post-internship independent productization

- Rebuilt PRD V2 into **24 reviewable requirements** and **36 Given-When-Then acceptance cases**.
- Added abnormal-path strategy, entity model, sequence diagrams and **35 tracking-event definitions**.
- Built a responsive prototype covering login, registration and recovery; browser checks improved from **13/15 to 15/15** after fixing OTP-state leakage.

## AI-assisted recovery concept

Designed a later concept in which AI pre-screens recovery materials through field extraction, completeness checks and risk routing while preserving human final decisions. This is a post-internship product proposal, not an internship-period launch claim.
